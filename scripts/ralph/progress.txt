## 2026-09-02 - WEB-SEC-026 (MFA bypassed on web)
- The story's proposed fix was client-side and would not have closed the hole:
  an aal1 token from signInWithPassword is a valid GoTrue credential and works
  against PostgREST and every edge function without our app.
- Server-side control added at the real chokepoint: _shared/mfaAssurance.ts
  (readAalClaim / hasVerifiedFactor / mfaAssuranceSatisfied) + SecurityContext
  .mfaSatisfied + a Layer 1b refusal (MFA_REQUIRED) in securityMiddleware,
  ahead of the role checks and scoped to elevated calls.
- Client: handleAuthChange derives isAuthenticated from the assurance level;
  Auth.tsx gates its redirect; handleMFACancel now awaits logout().
- Tests: _tests/mfa-assurance.test.ts (7, in CI) + 4 new vitest cases.
- **Learnings for future iterations:**
  - user_roles client writes are ALREADY denied outright by 20260612000000
    (WITH CHECK false). Any story proposing new RLS on user_roles writes is
    proposing dead code; role changes go through the assign-role edge function.
  - A RESTRICTIVE policy is table-wide and cannot distinguish an admin path from
    an owner writing their own row. On a table like campaigns that means
    blocking paying users, not admins.
  - requireAdmin -> securityMiddleware in _shared/securityLayers.ts is the one
    door every admin edge function goes through. Put caller-identity rules there.
  - getAuthenticatorAssuranceLevel() is a LOCAL decode of the stored session in
    supabase-js v2, not a network call, so it is safe to call on every auth event.
  - Watch out for guard conditions like `if (!text.includes('mfaAssurance.ts'))`
    when the edit you just made added a COMMENT mentioning that filename: the
    guard sees its own prose and skips the import. deno check caught it.
  - securityLayers.ts has imports at line 11 AND line 569; both are real.
