    in subscription-sync-tests.yml, so a new test must be added there or it
    never runs. `npm i -g deno@2.9.6` works in this container.

    route-smoke|cookie-consent, so new smoke tests go into route-smoke.spec.ts.

    probed. Record expected outcomes and leave the owner a one-line action.
