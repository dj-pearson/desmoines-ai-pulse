## 2026-08-28 - a11y sweep: one real defect, two false positives, one impossible check
- /restaurants shipped 3 empty <a class="block" href="..."></a>. Cause: RestaurantOpenings nested a
  "Read More" <a> inside a card-wide <Link>. Chromium splits nested anchors; the empty outer one is
  what ships. Fixed with the stretched-link pattern. Only component in src/ with the nesting.
- FALSE POSITIVES I nearly filed: 29 "unnamed buttons" on /stay are Radix checkboxes and <button> is a
  labelable element, so their <label for> names them. The "unlabelled inputs" were Radix's hidden
  native <select aria-hidden="true">.

  querySelectorAll('a a') matches nothing in dist/ even on a broken page. Deleted it and wrote down
  why; the empty anchor it leaves behind is the detectable trace, so the unnamed-link rule IS the
  nested-anchor check.
- **Learnings for future iterations:**
  - CONTROL EVERY ASSERTION, INCLUDING THE ONES THAT LOOK OBVIOUS. This one read as solid protection
    and could never trigger. A check nobody controls is a comment.

    anchors and leaves an empty one; assert on the trace, not the cause.
