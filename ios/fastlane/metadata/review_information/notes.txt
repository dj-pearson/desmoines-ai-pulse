REVIEW NOTES — Des Moines Insider 1.2.0

Thank you for reviewing. This update brings the app to feature parity with our
web product (AI Trip Planner, guides, deals, content hubs, dashboard) and adds
two monetization paths. Notes on the review-sensitive areas:

1. CONSUMER SUBSCRIPTIONS (Apple In-App Purchase)
   - Insider and VIP memberships are auto-renewable subscriptions sold through
     StoreKit / Apple IAP only (subscription group 21957951).
   - They unlock app features for the purchasing user: AI Trip Planner, saved
     searches & custom alerts, unlimited favorites, writing reviews, ad-free
     browsing, and exclusive content.
   - Restore Purchases and links to our Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy
     are on the subscription screen.
   - Cross-platform entitlements: a user who already subscribed on our website
     or Android is honored on iOS so they are never charged twice. We never
     direct users to an external purchase flow for these digital memberships.

2. ADVERTISER / "PROMOTE" FLOW (NOT a digital good for the app user)
   - Some screens show a "Promote your business" / advertiser entry point.
   - This is a business-to-business service for local venues to buy promotional
     placement. It is a real-world advertising service, not a digital good or
     feature consumed inside the app by the end user, so per App Store Review
     Guideline 3.1.3(e)/3.1.5(a) it is handled outside IAP. The button links
     out to our web advertiser portal in an in-app browser; no purchase is made
     inside the app. The typical app user never sees a charge here.

3. SPONSORED / AD CONTENT LABELING
   - Free-tier users may see ads and sponsored listings. All such content is
     clearly labeled: banner ads carry an "Ad" tag, and sponsored/featured
     listings and deals carry a "Sponsored" badge (with a matching VoiceOver
     label, e.g. "Sponsored content"). Paid subscribers see no ads.

4. DEMO ACCOUNT FOR PREMIUM REVIEW
   - A demo account with an active premium (VIP) entitlement is provided in the
     "Sign-In Information" fields of this App Review submission so you can review
     all gated features without purchasing. (Credentials are entered directly in
     App Store Connect and are intentionally not stored in our source repo.)
   - To exercise IAP itself, use the StoreKit sandbox on the subscription screen.

5. ACCESSIBILITY & OFFLINE
   - The app supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type up to AX5, and Reduce Motion.
   - Screens degrade gracefully offline (cached content + retry), so a flaky or
     absent network shows cached data or a retry affordance rather than a blank.

Contact us via the support URL in this submission with any questions. Thanks!
