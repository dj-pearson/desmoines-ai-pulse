## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Des Moines Insider is your all-in-one guide to discovering the best of Des Moines, Iowa. Whether you're a local looking for something new or a visitor planning your trip, we've got you covered.

**Discover Events**
Browse hundreds of local events — concerts, food festivals, art shows, outdoor activities, and more. Filter by category, date, and distance to find exactly what you're looking for. Featured events and urgency labels help you never miss what matters.

**Find Great Restaurants**
Explore Des Moines' dining scene with detailed restaurant listings. Filter by cuisine, price range, and rating. See real-time open/closed status and get directions with one tap.

**Explore Attractions**
Discover museums, parks, landmarks, and hidden gems across the metro area. View them on an interactive map and plan your visit.

**Interactive Map**
See events, restaurants, and attractions plotted on a map near you. Color-coded pins make it easy to find what you want. Tap any pin for details and navigation.

**Save Your Favorites**
Bookmark events and restaurants to revisit later. Your favorites sync across devices so you never lose track.

**Premium Features (Insider & VIP)**
Upgrade to unlock advanced filters, AI-powered trip planning, insider tips, calendar integration, ad-free browsing, and more.

**Key Features:**
- 6-tab navigation: Home, Dining, Search, Map, Saved, Profile
- Full-text and fuzzy search across all content types
- Offline support with smart caching
- Dark mode support
- TalkBack accessibility
- Push notifications for event reminders
- Deep linking to any event, restaurant, or attraction
- Google Sign-In support

Download Des Moines Insider today and start exploring!
