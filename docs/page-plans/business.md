## Where it stands

- The price on `/advertise` isn't the price Stripe charges, for two separate reasons. Each placement has its own "Days" input defaulting to 7 (`Advertise.tsx:98`, input at `:465-478`) and the summary totals that number (`:118-124`), while the pricing trigger and `create-campaign-checkout` both derive days from the campaign dates (`20260902000008:76-82`, `create-campaign-checkout/index.ts:245-252`). And the formula the page mirrors is gone: `placementTotalPrice` copies the TEXT overload of `calculate_campaign_pricing` from `20260227000000:239`, which `20260822000004:148` dropped (that migration is in the snapshot ledger). What survives takes the `placement_type` enum and returns `base_price`/`demand_multiplier` (`types.ts:13192-13203`); its body isn't in the repo. Checkout prices with the survivor (`index.ts:255-258`), so the page and the charge use two formulas. That's WEB-ADS-003 again.
- The WEB-ADS-003 trigger itself calls `calculate_campaign_pricing(NEW.placement_type::text, ...)` (`20260902000008:88`). With only the enum overload left, that's a 42883 on every non-admin placement insert the moment `20260902000008` is applied. It isn't in the snapshot ledger; whether it has been applied since is unknown, and if it has, nobody can buy an ad today. The Deno test at `supabase/functions/_tests/campaign-pricing-authority.test.ts:36` asserts the `::text` call, so it locks the bug in.
- A campaign that wasn't paid for can't be paid for. `createCheckoutSession` has one caller, `Advertise.tsx:189`, straight after `createCampaign`. The dashboard's "Complete Payment" goes to `/campaigns/:id` (`CampaignDashboard.tsx:163-171`), which has no `pending_payment` branch (`CampaignDetail.tsx:174-202`). Cancel's retry goes to `/campaigns` (`AdvertiseCancel.tsx:15-20`). "Complete Setup" opens a blank `/advertise` and makes a second draft. Renew creates a draft that says "pay to start it" with nowhere to pay. The Account plan's `AdvertiseTab` also links "Complete payment" here, assuming this page owns checkout.
- The pages state things nothing measures or does: "50K+ Monthly Visitors", "15K+ Newsletter Subscribers", "95% Local Audience" (`Advertise.tsx:228-236`) directly above `PlatformMetrics`, the component written to stop exactly that; "Reach thousands" in the meta and OG tags (`:205,:208,:213,:224`); a 555 phone number, staffed hours and a 4-hour response time (`:781`); "Demographics", "geographic data" and hourly reports (`:756,:763`); a CPM "impressions/day" that is price divided by CPM (`:445-505`), with admin copy shown to buyers (`:495`). The hub adds "30% higher engagement" (`BusinessHub.tsx:274`), targeting by "interests and behavior" (`:242`) and a $99/$299/$599 monthly package grid whose "Select Package" button has no handler (`BusinessPartnership.tsx:194-231`), a second price list next to `ad_rate_card`. `/submit-event` promises review "within 48 hours" three times (`SubmitEvent.tsx:23,63,117`).
- Some features are shells. Team invites toast "An invitation has been sent" (`useTeamManagement.ts:117-120`) when nothing sends mail, and every row renders blank because snake_case rows are cast to a camelCase interface (`:47`, rendered at `TeamManagement.tsx:199-213`). `/business` centres on a `business_profiles` row that no reachable path creates (`BusinessHub.tsx:179-202`), and its analytics read `business_analytics`, which nothing writes. Campaign analytics count raw `ad_impressions` rows in the browser (`useCampaignAnalytics.tsx:50-72`), so any campaign past PostgREST's row cap under-reports the thing the advertiser paid for.
- Three server gaps sit under all of it. `send-campaign-notification` takes the recipient, title and body from the request (`index.ts:84-99,172-221`) and only checks that the caller owns some campaign (`decision.ts:58`), so a free draft is enough to send any text from the site's noreply address to any inbox. The public `ad-creatives` bucket still grants owners INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE on their campaign folder (`RLS_AUDIT.md:557,562,567`), which is where approved artwork is published, so it can be swapped after review. No migration guards `campaigns` or `campaign_creatives` columns, and `CreativeUploadForm.tsx:28-31` records that owners could once write `campaigns.status` from the browser.
- Worth keeping: `create-campaign-checkout`'s server repricing and its 409 on a mismatch, the WEB-ADS-007 compensating delete, `UPLOADABLE_STATUSES`, pause/cancel/renew/refund as owner-checked RPCs, the private review bucket, `PlatformMetrics`' no-fallback rule and `MIN_DATA_DAYS`, `ListingPicker`'s prefill, `useBusinessClaim`'s 42P01 degradation, triage failing closed, and the route-mocked specs `advertise-success-receipt` and `campaign-self-service`.

## What makes it ours

1. **The number on the page is the server's number.** The `/advertise` summary asks `calculate_campaign_pricing` itself, for the days the dates actually span, through the same RPC `create-campaign-checkout` prices with (`index.ts:255`) and `useCampaigns.getCurrentPricing` already calls from the browser (`useCampaigns.ts:224`). No mirror, no second formula. Yelp Ads and Eventbrite Boost sell estimated budgets; we can print "the total you see is the total you're charged" and mean it, because checkout refuses (409) to charge anything else.
2. **Honest inventory.** Each placement says where it actually appears, generated from the `<AdBanner placement=...>` call sites (`Index.tsx:153`, `EventsPage.tsx:525`, `Restaurants.tsx:880`, ...) and pinned by a test, with "From $X/day" from `ad_rate_card`. No audience claims. Measured delivery per placement (impressions served last 30 days, from `ad_impressions`) appears once its RPC is applied and only above `PlatformMetrics`' sample floor. Catch Des Moines' media kit and Yelp sell on reach claims; we show what the slot delivered or nothing.
3. **Claim, fix, promote, in one place.** `/business` becomes the signed-in workspace built on things that exist: your claimed listings (`business_claims`, `claim_listing` and the `update_claimed_listing` column whitelist in `20260920000005`, read the way `useBusinessClaim.ts` does), your submitted events (`useUserSubmittedEvents`, Account-owned, read-only here) and your campaigns. Each listing and each live event gets "Promote this" into `/advertise` with the listing already chosen. Today `ClaimListingCta.tsx:49` emits `?listing_type=&listing_id=` while `Advertise.tsx:81-90` reads `listingType`/`listingId`/`listingName`, so the link lands on an empty form. Nobody else lets a Des Moines owner fix their listing and pay to promote it in one sitting.
4. **A delivery report that says what it counts.** Exact impression and click counts, paid days (`campaign_placements.days_count`) next to days actually served (distinct `ad_impressions.date`), and a short "What we count" note taken from the code: 50% of the ad on screen for one second (`useAdTracking.ts:39-40`), bot user agents dropped (`supabase/functions/_shared/adEventFilters.ts`), app traffic not yet counted (`track-ad-event/index.ts:31-35`). Local sellers report opaque totals.

## How the packages fit together

WP0 is small and lands first, but its exports are specified here exactly, so WP1-WP4 can be written against them in parallel in one tree.
