## Where it stands

The plumbing from pass one holds. What doesn't hold is mostly the words on top of it, plus the edge shell nobody reached.

- **Trust labels that measure the wrong thing.** The detail FAQ, which ships as FAQPage JSON-LD, calls an `is_featured` row "an editor's pick" (`RestaurantDetails.tsx:547`), yet `20260902000004:20-24` records that nothing editorial writes that flag on restaurants and only sponsored rows kept it. The hub pill (`Restaurants.tsx:655-668`), card badge (`RestaurantCard.tsx:225-229`) and detail hero repeat it as "Featured". "New This Week" is scrape date (`RestaurantCard.tsx:163-167`). "Popular" is `popularity_score > 70` (`:169`), a formula of rating, the featured flag and recency. The FAQ adds "one of the highest-rated", "one of the most affordable" and "a popular dining destination" (`:512`, `:528`, `:547`), and the footer says "Verified" whenever `updated_at` moved in the last 7 days (`LastUpdatedBadge.tsx:41`).
- **Time frozen into static HTML.** `/restaurants/open-now`, `/restaurants` and `/breweries` are prerendered (`scripts/prerender-routes.mjs:52-53,87`). The open-now clock, "N of M open right now", every card's "Open until 10 PM" and the hub's dinner strip get baked in at build time. `isPrerender()` exists for this and seven components already use it; none of the restaurant ones do.
- **The edge shell is a pass behind React.** `RESTAURANT_SHELL_COLUMNS` (`_middleware.ts:115-116`) has no `status`, `is_merged` or `merged_into`, so closed and merged rows get a 200, a self-canonical and `openingHoursSpecification`. `menu_url` and `website` go into `href` through `escapeHtml` only (`:485-486`). A failed PostgREST read becomes `[]` (`:130-132`), then a cached 404 with noindex.
- **Rows are getting worse upstream.** `restaurant-opening-scraper` overwrites description, cuisine, location, phone, website and price on every matched row, because its lookup never selects the fields it checks (`index.ts:279`, `:356-363`). `data-quality-heal` selects `restaurants.address`, which doesn't exist (`index.ts:28`; not in the snapshot), so its nightly run fails. The admin Places tool stores every enum price level as "$" (`GooglePlacesRestaurantTools.tsx:362`).
- **Worth keeping:** URL-synced filters, the bounded RPC, the honest ItemList, fixture-backed smoke specs, `restaurantMeta.ts` as the one title builder shared with the edge, `resolveReservation`'s evidence rule, the dated openings labels, the dietary page's keyword-match candour, the brewery one-dialog pattern, and the code comments that record why each removed claim went.

## What makes it ours

Each bet uses data and code the app already has. None needs a new table, column or RPC.

1. **"Open at..." with filters that compose.** `/restaurants/open-now` already holds every row with hours in memory (`useOpenNowRestaurants.ts`, limit 600), and `getRestaurantOpenStatus` takes any instant. Add a time select (now, 9 PM, 10 PM, 11 PM, midnight), cuisine and city chips from the loaded rows, "open 1+ more hour" (`minutesUntilClose`), near me, and the named areas from `EVENT_AREAS` (`src/lib/eventAreas.ts`, read-only) once `latitude, longitude` join `OPEN_NOW_COLUMNS` (both in the snapshot). "Vegan, open at 10:30 PM, East Village" becomes a shareable URL with no new query. Yelp and Google answer for this minute; Catch Des Moines has no hours logic. Files: `useOpenNowRestaurants.ts`, `OpenNowRestaurants.tsx`. WP4.
2. **Find the dish, then check it's open.** `search_menu_items` is in production (`db-snapshot.json` functions; `20260317000001:140-186`) and returns `restaurant_slug`, `item_name` and `price`. On open-now, "Who serves pho" runs it once, drops merged and closed rows, and crosses the rest with the evaluator: "Pho tai $13 at X, open until 9 PM". It hides itself when the menu table has nothing for the term. Files: new `src/hooks/useDishSearch.ts`, `OpenNowRestaurants.tsx`. WP4.
3. **An openings ledger with receipts.** Every line is a dated label ("Opened Sep 12", "Opening Oct 2026") plus a "Source" link from `source_url` (already in `RESTAURANT_LIST_COLUMNS`) through `toSafeExternalUrl`. Stale announcements move to "Announced, not confirmed" with the original timeframe, and `/restaurants/new` opens with "N added since your last visit" (timestamp in `safeStorage`). One definition of "new" (opening date or `newly_opened` in a window, never `created_at`) serves the hub, `/new` and the card. Files: `restaurantOpenings.ts`, `RestaurantOpenings.tsx`, `NewRestaurants.tsx`, `RestaurantCard.tsx`. WP2.
4. **Where each fact came from.** The detail page drops the blanket "Verified" and labels facts with their real source and date: "Google rating", "Menu captured Aug 3 from their site" (`restaurant_menus.captured_at`), "Hours from Google, checked <date>" when a row carries `hours_json.fetchedAt` (read through `select('*')`, so nothing breaks while the column is missing). AI text is labelled as AI wherever it appears. Files: `RestaurantDetails.tsx`, `RestaurantMenuSection.tsx`. WP3.
5. **Your evening from the restaurant page.** "Tonight nearby" moves from last place to right under the hours, with the restaurant's own "Open until 10 PM CT" beside each event's start time, and gets a chip in the section nav. It uses `useTonightNearRestaurant` and the `tonightPairings.ts` formatters (read-only). Yelp has the hours and Eventbrite has the events; neither joins them. Files: `RestaurantDetails.tsx`, `NearbyContent.tsx`, `useTonightNearRestaurant.ts`. WP3.

Not adopted: "live music here tonight" on cards (matching `events.venue` to `restaurants.name` is the same fragile join as the brewery strip), and menu-version diffs (useful, but no shipped reader to build on). Both can come back once venue matching is by coordinates.

## How the packages fit together

WP1-WP5 own disjoint files and can run in parallel in one tree. **WP6 lands last** and is the only package that edits `playwright.smoke.config.ts`, `.github/workflows/e2e.yml`, `.github/e2e-lane-baseline.json` and `.github/workflows/subscription-sync-tests.yml`. - WP5 adds `src/lib/safeUrl.ts`. `src/lib/reservations.ts` (WP5) delegates to it, so WP3's existing `safeWebUrl` calls pick it up with no edit.
