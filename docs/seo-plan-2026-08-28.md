# Des Moines Insider — SEO plan, 2026-08-28

Written against two inputs: the Search Console export for the last 16 months
(`Keyword/desmoinesinsider.com-Performance-on-Search-2026-08-28/`) and a Keyword Planner pull of
3,501 terms (`Keyword/Keyword Stats 2026-08-28 at 23_44_07.csv`). Every number below was measured
from those files or from a live fetch of the site on 2026-08-28. Where a figure is a floor or an
estimate, it says so.

**Read the Keyword Planner volumes as buckets, not counts.** Without an active spending ad account
Google returns 500,000 / 50,000 / 5,000 / 500 / 50 and nothing in between. The distribution here is
10 / 35 / 221 / 776 / 2,130 / 329-at-zero, which is the bucketing, not a real curve. Volumes in this
document are order-of-magnitude only and are never used to compute a traffic forecast.

13.8.** The last twelve days run about 9 clicks and 1,150 impressions a day at position 13-14, so
this is a settled state rather than a dip.

Restaurants is 76% of all impressions. Attractions, one of the two categories the competition is
built around, has produced one click in 16 months.

## 2. The finding that reorders everything else

Split the top 1,000 queries by intent. Does the searcher want a specific named place, or are they
still deciding?

| Intent | Queries | Clicks | Impressions | CTR | Weighted position |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Branded / navigational | 781 | 773 | 87,019 | 0.89% | **13.5** |
| Discovery | 219 | 116 | 12,601 | 0.92% | **23.8** |

Then check the terms the site is nominally built to win:

| Query | Impressions | Clicks | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| things to do in des moines | 51 | 0 | 39.8 |
| des moines events | 177 | 0 | 31.7 |
| des moines attractions | 52 | 0 | 40.1 |
| des moines restaurants | 629 | 0 | 52.2 |
| best restaurants in des moines | 231 | 1 | 50.2 |
| things to do this weekend in des moines | 25 | 0 | 33.5 |
| places to eat in des moines | 117 | 0 | 56.9 |
| des moines events this weekend | 84 | 0 | 37.2 |
| fun things to do in des moines | 38 | 0 | 48.7 |

**Every core term ranks between 30 and 57 and every one of them has zero clicks.**

Meanwhile the site sits at position 7-11 for "bonchon des moines menu" (2,010 impressions, 3 clicks),
"the pizza bar menu" (1,203 impressions, 1 click), "dutch bros des moines" (1,917 impressions, 11
clicks) and hundreds like them. That traffic converts at 0.15% because it never could: somebody
searching a restaurant by name wants that restaurant's own page or its Google listing, and a
third-party directory is the wrong answer to their question no matter how well it's built.

So the shape of the problem is not "low CTR". It's that **entity pages have enough authority to rank
on page one while the hub pages that should be the front door sit on page three to six.** That is
backwards for a directory, and it's the thing to fix.

Catch Des Moines has no structured data anywhere on its three main hubs. It isn't beating us on

Two things follow. We are not going to out-authority the CVB this quarter, so the plan doesn't try
to. And **structured data is an open lane** — event rich results, and citation by AI search, are
both available to whoever does the work, and the incumbent has done none of it.

## 4. The technical faults, in the order they cost us

### 4.1 Every entity page serves the homepage to crawlers that don't run JavaScript

Fetched as GPTBot on 2026-08-28:

```
/restaurants/atlas-caf                 title: "Things to Do in Des Moines This Weekend"  ld+json: NONE
/restaurants/marvs-mainstreet-dive     title: "Things to Do in Des Moines This Weekend"  ld+json: NONE
/attractions/pappajohn-sculpture-park  title: "Things to Do in Des Moines This Weekend"  ld+json: NONE
/events/touch-a-truck-2026-05-22       title: "Things to Do in Des Moines This Weekend"  ld+json: NONE
```

The H1 on all four is "Good Afternoon! Find things to do in Des Moines, right now", which is the
homepage hero. The canonical is correct, and nothing else is.

The cause is in the build. `scripts/prerender.mjs` renders the 43 hub routes in `PRERENDER_ROUTES`
and gates the entity pass behind `PRERENDER_ENTITIES=true`, which is off. So roughly **1,070 of the
1,109 URLs in our own sitemaps are, to a JS-less crawler, near-identical copies of the homepage.**

Googlebot renders JavaScript, which is why restaurants still earn 180k impressions. GPTBot,
OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and Applebot-Extended do not. `robots.txt`
invites all of them by name. We invite them to 1,070 copies of the same page.

That gate was set to fail closed for a real reason, recorded in the script's own header: on a build
with an unreachable database every entity URL rendered the same ~1,370 characters of boilerplate,
and publishing those would have replaced a page Google renders properly with static thin content.
The fix is to satisfy the existing per-page validation, not to remove it.

### 4.2 Event rich results have collapsed from 72 valid to 6

From the Search Console Events report already committed at
`desmoinesinsider.com-Events-2026-03-08/`:

- Peak 72 valid items in December, still 57 on 2026-03-02, then **6** from 2026-03-03 onward.
- Zero critical issues. Nine non-critical, each on 3 items: missing `endDate`, `image`,
  `eventStatus`, `description`, `performer`, `organizer`, and `availability` / `validFrom` / `url`
  inside `offers`.

We publish 462 event URLs and Google credits 6 of them with a rich result. The Search Appearance
export for the whole 16 months is **empty** — not one row — which is consistent with the site earning
no enhanced result of any kind.

Sampling the `Event` objects our `/events` ItemList emits confirms the gap: name, startDate,
location, image, url and description are present; `endDate`, `eventStatus`, `eventAttendanceMode`,
`offers`, `performer` and `organizer` are absent. `eventStatus` and `eventAttendanceMode` have been
effectively required since 2020.

One data bug surfaced while reading that sample: a Waukee venue is emitted with
`addressLocality: "Des Moines"`. The locality looks hardcoded rather than read from the venue.

### 4.3 FAQ schema is invalid on 30 pages

Same folder, FAQ report: **30 invalid against 8 valid, and the critical issue is
`Duplicate field "FAQPage"`**, meaning two FAQPage blocks on one page. Seventeen source files
reference FAQ schema, and several pages compose both a page-level FAQ and a component that emits its
own.

Worth being straight about the value. Google restricted FAQ rich results to government and health
sites in August 2023, and the report shows 0 impressions from this enhancement across the window.
Fixing it wins no clicks directly. It's worth doing because an invalid block is noise in every future
validation run, and because the FAQ content itself is useful to AI search, which reads JSON-LD
whether or not Google draws a widget.

### 4.4 Trailing-slash duplicates are indexed as separate URLs

`/restaurants` (20,789 impressions, position 24.4) and `/restaurants/` (5,401 impressions, position
25.1) are both in the index. Same for `/events` and `/events/`, `/events/date-night` and
`/events/date-night/`, `/playgrounds` and `/playgrounds/`, `/stay` and `/stay/`.

Both forms return 200. The canonical on the slashed version correctly points at the unslashed one, so
Google will consolidate eventually, but it's currently splitting impressions across two URLs on our
worst-performing pages. A 301 settles it immediately.

### 4.5 Duplicate entity records

`/restaurants/bonchon` and `/restaurants/bonchon-2` are both indexed and both earn clicks (87 and
33). `/restaurants/daves-hot-chicken-2` likewise. The `-2` suffix is the slug generator working
around a duplicate row.

### 4.6 Brand name inconsistency

`/events/date-night` serves the title `... | Des Moines AI Pulse`. `robots.txt` opens with
`# Des Moines AI Pulse`. The product is Des Moines Insider. Old branding is leaking into the two
places a machine reads first.

### 4.7 Desktop ranks 12 positions worse than mobile

Mobile: 1,639 clicks, 136,583 impressions, position 9.48. Desktop: 629 clicks, 96,689 impressions,
position **21.41**. A gap that wide is usually layout or Core Web Vitals at the desktop breakpoint,
and it hasn't been diagnosed. Recorded here as a question to answer, not a fix to apply.

## 5. Strategy

Three moves, in dependency order. Nothing in phase two pays off until phase one lands.

**Fix what the crawler sees.** Turn the entity prerender on and pass its validation, complete the
Event schema, kill the duplicate FAQPage, 301 the trailing slashes. This phase has the largest gap
between effort and payoff, because the content already exists and simply isn't being served to
anything that doesn't run JavaScript.

**Rebuild the hubs to win discovery terms.** Every hub gets real editorial depth, an internal link
count in the competition's range rather than a third of it, and a schema type that matches what it
is. `/things-to-do` at 597 words is the thinnest page on the site and it targets the single highest
value term in the set.

**Then go after the query space we don't cover at all.** Weekend and today pages, seasonal, kids and
family, free, date night, neighborhood, venue and concert. The routes for most of these already exist
and are prerendered; they have no content worth ranking.

What this plan deliberately doesn't do is chase more branded restaurant impressions. That's 87,019
impressions converting at 0.89% and it's the wrong traffic. Those pages stay, they get correct schema
so they can be cited, and they stop being the strategy.

## 6. Per-module plan

### Homepage

Position 30.03 with 12 clicks in 16 months, targeting nothing in particular. The title reads "Things
to Do in Des Moines This Weekend" while `/events/this-weekend` and `/weekend` target that same
phrase, so the site's strongest page competes with two of its own.

- Re-target the homepage on the brand plus the city entity, and let the hubs own their head terms.
- The hero H1 currently reads "Good Afternoon! Find things to do in Des Moines, right now". The
  greeting is JavaScript state that ends up in the prerendered HTML and in every entity page's shell.
  Take it out of the H1.
- Raise internal links from 54 toward 150+, into all hubs, the top neighborhoods and the current
  weekend.
- Keep `WebSite` + `Organization` + `LocalBusiness`; add `SearchAction` wired to `/search`.

### Events, the highest-value module

462 URLs, 30,646 impressions, and 6 valid rich results. "des moines events" is a 5,000 bucket at High
competition and we're position 31.7.

- Complete `Event` schema on detail pages and in every ItemList: `endDate`, `eventStatus`,
  `eventAttendanceMode`, `offers` with `url` / `availability` / `validFrom`, `organizer`, `performer`,
  `description`, `image`. Fix the hardcoded `addressLocality`.
- Prerender all 462 event pages so ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can read and cite them.
- `/events` hub: an editorial intro that says what's actually on in Des Moines, this month's
  highlights, links to every sub-route and venue, 250+ internal links.
- `/events/this-weekend` and `/events/today` are the recurring-traffic pages. Both need a visible
  freshness date, and `/weekend` should 301 to `/events/this-weekend` rather than duplicate it.
- Build the month pages the `MonthlyEventsPage` route already supports. "des moines events september
  2026" and its eleven siblings are how people search a calendar.

### Restaurants, stop optimizing for the wrong query

408 pages, 180,254 impressions, 0.95%. `/restaurants` is position 24.4 for its own head term and
position 52.2 for "des moines restaurants".

- The hub is the priority, not the detail pages. Rework `/restaurants` around "best restaurants in
  des moines" with actual editorial picks and reasons, not a filtered grid.
- Add `Restaurant` schema to detail pages so they're eligible to be cited, and prerender them, but
  don't invest further in ranking them.
- Build the cuisine and occasion pages the query set asks for and the routes half-support: steakhouse,
  italian, mexican, brunch, breakfast, patio, rooftop, happy hour, fine dining, new restaurants.
  "new restaurants des moines 2026" already earns 7.63% CTR at position 7.2, the best-converting query
  on the site, and it's currently served by a detail page rather than a hub.
- Merge the `-2` duplicates and redirect.

### Attractions, one click in 16 months

22 URLs in the sitemap, 10 pages with impressions, 760 impressions, 1 click. "des moines attractions"
is a 50,000 bucket at High competition and we're position 40.1.

This is the largest untouched opportunity on the site and currently its weakest module.

- Rebuild `/attractions` as a real guide. 22 attractions isn't a city's worth; Catch Des Moines covers
  considerably more.
- `TouristAttraction` schema on every detail page. The component exists and isn't reaching crawlers.
- Cover the venue and landmark queries the planner shows: Iowa Events Center, Civic Center, Val Air
  Ballroom, Wells Fargo Arena, Blank Park Zoo, Pappajohn Sculpture Park, Botanical Garden.

### Things to Do, thinnest page and biggest term

597 words against a 50,000-bucket High-competition term, position 39.8, 29 clicks.

- This should be the deepest editorial page on the site and it's the shallowest.
- Rebuild as the top-of-funnel hub: by season, by audience, by neighborhood, by budget, by weather,
  each linking down into events, attractions, restaurants and outdoors.
- The `/things-to-do/:seg1/:seg2` route already exists, and `festivals`, `budget`, `east-village`,
  `ankeny` and `fall` already earn impressions with no real content behind them.

### Stay, 6,726 impressions and 2 clicks

70 pages at 0.03% CTR. `/stay` itself is position 52.7 with zero clicks on 2,718 impressions.

This module is a decision, not a task. Hotel search is owned by Booking, Expedia and Google's own
hotel panel, and a directory doesn't break into it. Three honest options: invest properly in "hotels
near Iowa Events Center" style event-adjacent intent where we have a real angle, cut back to a handful
of guide pages, or noindex it and stop spending crawl budget. The recommendation is the first, scoped
tightly to event-adjacent queries, because that's the one place we know something Booking doesn't.

### Playgrounds, quietly the best-performing module

47 pages, 6,706 impressions, several pages at position 6-7. `riverview-park` alone has 1,366
impressions. Nobody else covers this well.

- Cheap wins available: complete schema, prerender, and add the parent hub content.
- Extend into the family and kids query cluster, which the planner shows and the site barely touches.

### Articles, works, and there are only 17

`/articles/the-ultimate-des-moines-patio-guide` earns 13 clicks at position 14.9; the pumpkin patch
guide has 2,609 impressions. Seasonal editorial ranks.

- 18 URLs is not a content programme. This is the lever for the discovery terms the hubs can't reach
  directly.
- Publish against the seasonal cluster on a schedule, and link every article into the relevant hub in
  both directions.

### pSEO

18 URLs live from a system built for far more (`src/pseo/` has taxonomy, schemas, prompts and a
pipeline). Once hub quality is fixed, this is the mechanism for neighborhood and cuisine coverage at
volume. It shouldn't be scaled before the hubs are fixed, or it multiplies a thin template.

## 7. How this gets measured

Traffic forecasts from bucketed volume data would be invented, so there are none here. The
measurements that matter, all already available:

- **Valid Event rich results**, from the Search Console Events report. Baseline 6. The clearest single
  number in the whole plan.
- **Position on the nine core terms in section 2.** Baseline 30-57, zero clicks. Any of them reaching
  page two is real movement.
- **Discovery-intent CTR and weighted position**, recomputed from the same Queries export the same
  way. Baseline 0.92% and 23.8.
- **Entity pages serving their own title to a JS-less crawler.** Baseline 0 of ~1,070. This one is
  verifiable with curl on the day it ships and needs no waiting.

Deliberately not a metric: total impressions. It sits at 236,225 while the site earns 2,293 clicks, so
it can rise on more of the wrong traffic and mean nothing.

### Four things the plan got wrong

Recorded because the corrections are more useful than the original claims.

**`eventStatus` and `eventAttendanceMode` were never missing.** Section 4.2 says they're absent
everywhere. They're present on 30/30, 40/40 and 23/23 items across the three event hubs. The claim
came from a JSON dump truncated at 900 characters. The real defect was narrower and more interesting:
`/events` had no `endDate` while its two siblings did, from the same rows, because a second copy of
the builder had never gained the field.

**`/weekend` was already a working 301.** Section 6 asks for it. It has been redirecting since
WEB-SEO-007. The original probe used `curl -L`, which reports the status after following.

**The `-2` slugs are not duplicates.** Section 4.5 calls them duplicate records. Both surviving pairs
are real second locations: Texas Roadhouse in Johnston and West Des Moines, Flip'N Jacks in Des
Moines and Altoona. A 301 was staged and reverted. What they needed was distinct titles.

**`MonthlyEventsPage` is routed.** It looked unrouted because App.tsx imports it and never uses the
import; `EventsSegmentHandler` imports it separately and dispatches month slugs to it. The real
problem was that nothing linked to or sitemapped those URLs.

### Three things found that the plan never mentioned

**The strict gate did not exist.** `prerender.mjs` documented it, threaded a `strict` parameter
through `renderPool` into `renderRoute`, and never read it. `shellTitle` was computed and never read.
It was the only named guard behind keeping entity prerendering off, so ~1,070 URLs served the
homepage to avoid a risk whose mitigation was a comment.

**FAQ schema was invalid for a second reason nobody had recorded.** Six pages declared an FAQ that
rendered nowhere on the page, which Google's guidance disallows independently of the duplication. A
fix aimed only at the duplicate count would have left every surviving block non-compliant.

**Three `/things-to-do` sub-pages earn impressions with no published page.** `/things-to-do/festivals`
(772 impressions, position 17.1), `/east-village` (399) and `/ankeny` (383) are taxonomy-valid slugs
with no pSEO page, so they 404 after JS while the SPA fallback serves the homepage shell at 200 to
crawlers. This is the cheapest remaining win: the demand is measured, the route works, and what is
missing is a published page.

### Open, and why

Everything left needs either editorial content about real businesses or data this environment
cannot reach. The line held throughout: **no hours, prices, distances, capacities or opinions were
generated.** A wrong opening time sends somebody to a closed building.

- **SEO-010, 011, 012, 017, 019** need writing grounded in real knowledge of these places.
- **SEO-018** is the best-value item left and is mostly *data*, not prose: a venue page's worth is its
  upcoming-event list, which the events table can already produce. It needs venue-name normalisation
  over `events.venue`, the same field SEO-007 found carrying a hardcoded locality.
- **SEO-013** depends on SEO-018. "Which hotel is walkable to this venue" is a proximity join, and
  without the venue side it would mean inventing distances.
- **SEO-014** is largely done by SEO-001; what remains is hub content and cross-linking to
  `/events/kids`, which needs no new facts.
- **SEO-020** cannot be diagnosed from here. PageSpeed Insights refused both runs on quota with no API
  key, and the export cannot split queries by device. Test the cheap hypothesis first: pull Search
  Console's Queries dimension filtered by device. If the query sets differ, there is no defect.

### Two things that need a deploy before they can be called done

- **SEO-001**: that entity pages serve their own title to a JS-less crawler. Baseline 0 of ~1,070.
  Verifiable with `curl` on the day it ships.
- **SEO-002**: valid Event rich results, baseline 6. This one lags a Search Console re-crawl and
  cannot be confirmed on ship day.
